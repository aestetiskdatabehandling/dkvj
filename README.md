## DKVJ Database

Collection of knowlegde and ressources for the course on digital art at Engelsholm Højskole.

This database is meant to support students of the course, but in order to add a bit to the world of digital art, I've decided to make it accessible to a wider community on the Internet. Hopefully it will help someone to get started, or fix a problem they've encountered.

Everything I've written here is something I've told students at one point at least, since I started teaching in 2018. I might be wrong about one thing or another - let me know!
