# Digital hardware

## Aja HA5-4K
*HDMI in, 4x SDI out*
Digtal HDMI to SDI video splitter or video wall. Can be configured with software over USB-micro to either distribute the same image on all four outputs, or slice a 4K input into 4x FullHD outputs.

*Only works with a high quality HDMI 2.1 cable*

## RGBlink

### RGBLink mini v1
*4 HDMI inputs (1080p, 1 HDMI outputs (1080p), 1 USB output*
Cheap and utilitarian video mixer, capable of mixing two sources.
Can either do a straight mix or wipe, using either the T-bar or a set timing. Using a USB-A to USB-A cable (USB 3.0), the mixer doubles as a capture card.
While boring, the mixer is a very useful tool to ensure a working signal from multiple sources. It also scales a lot of different signals, including interlaced sources.

Notes:<br>
- Device is always-on, has no power switch.<br>
- For some reason (probably cost), the little preview screen is in 4:3, meaning that every input looks squished.
- Pressing buttons 2 and 3 simultaneously switches the HDMI output between Preview an Program. Don't do this while performing! <br>
- This device color converts from YbPbPr colorspace to RGB, meaning that it can "fix" weird colors between Macs and hardware that requires RGB.<br>
- Supposedly, the device has exactly the same hardware as the LivePro L1, and people have claimed that it is possible to flash the firmware with firmware for that device or the mini+, enabling luma keying as a mix mode.

### RGBLink mini v2
*4 HDMI inputs (2160p), 2 HDMI outputs (2160p), 1 USB output*

Same device as the mini v1, with higher resolutions and more features.
Main improvement are the double HDMI outputs, meaning you can add a second monitor for Preview output.
The device has a touchscreen with a lot of menu options.

Notes:
The device has a long boot time compared to its older sibling.
Output 1 has more routing options than Output 2.

# Analog hardware

## No brand stuff

### AV2HDMI
*Composite in (PAL/NTSC), HDMI out (720p/1080p)*
The cheapest no-brand analog-to-digital video converter. You get what you pay for.
Output will be stretched from 4:3 to 16:9 and will feature a few broken pixel-lines at the bottom of the image.

Use with 5V USB power.

---

## HDMI2AV
*HDMI in (720p/1080p), composite out (PAL/NTSC)*

The cheapest no-brand digital-to-analog video converter. You get what you pay for. 
HDMI input calls for 16:9 formats, but the output will be squished to 4:3.
Use with 5V USB power.
