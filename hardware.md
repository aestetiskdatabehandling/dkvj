# Analog hardware

## No brand stuff

### AV2HDMI
*Composite in (PAL/NTSC), HDMI out (720p/1080p)*
The cheapest no-brand analog-to-digital video converter. You get what you pay for.
Output will be stretched from 4:3 to 16:9 and will feature a few broken pixel-lines at the bottom of the image.

Use with 5V USB power.

---

## HDMI2AV
*HDMI in (720p/1080p), composite out (PAL/NTSC)*

The cheapest no-brand digital-to-analog video converter. You get what you pay for. 
HDMI input calls for 16:9 formats, but the output will be squished to 4:3.
Use with 5V USB power.
