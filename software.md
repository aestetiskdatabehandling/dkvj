# Software


## Avenue *// Resolume*
*paid software, video playback, video effects, audio playback* <br>

Arena stripped of features like layer groups and mapping. A capable entry-level VJing software.

---

## Alley *// Resolume*
*free software, video conversion* <br>

Video converter mainly used for converting to Resolumes own codec DXV.

---

## Arena *// Resolume*
*paid software, video playback, video effects, audio playback*

The gold standard for VJing, due to it's active development, crossplatform status and large community.

In the workshop we currently useuse version [Resolume Arena version 7.22.9](https://resolume.com/download/file?file=Resolume_Arena_7_22_9_rev_47596_No_Footage_Installer.exe)

---

## [Chataigne](https://github.com/benkuper/Chataigne)
*open source software, show control*

Made for controlling other software, Chataigne can be used as a central command center for processing MIDI, OSC, audio, DMX, SMPTE and a long list of other protocols. It can be used to synchronize two or more pieces of software (even between computers on a network), working as the "conductor".

---

### DaVinci Resolve *by BlackmagicDesign*
*free software, video editing* <br>

Resolve is BlackmagicDesigns colorgrading software turned full linear editing suite. They offer a free tier with restricted features. Most notably, their plug-and-play effects are not available, but most basic post-processing effects can be made using their node-based compositing

---

### Desktop Video Setup *// BlackmagicDesign*
*free software, video capture*

Utility for configuring BlackmagicDesigns own capture card hardware.

---


## [MadMapper](https://madmapper.com) *// GarageCube*
*paid software, projection mapping, video playback, audio playback, DMX*

The gold standard in projection mapping software.

## [Modul8](https://modul8.com) *// GarageCube*
*paid software, video playback*

VJing software by the team behind MadMapper. Not that used in the industry anymore.

**MacOS only!**

---

## FFmpeg *// the FFmpeg team*

*Open source software, command line interface*

FFmpeg is a project aimed at making video conversion and editing free for all. It is a very capable tool, but as it is based around the command line it will scare of some people.

For using FFmpeg with a more user-friendly interface, look to Shutter Encoder.

---

## [VDMX](https://vidvox.net) *// VidVox*

*paid software, video playback, video effects*

VJing software with a modular interface.

**MacOS only!** 

---

## BlackSyphon *// Vidvox*
*free software, capture*

Utility for routing signals from BlackmagicDesign through the Syphon protocol.

**MacOS only!**

---


## KDEnlive *// KDE*
*open source software, video editing* <br>

Full video editing suite, better and more stable than other open source competitors.

---

## Shutter Encoder *by Paul Pacifico*
*Open source software, video conversion, video download* <br>

Friendly interface for ffmpeg.

---


### [NDI Tools](https://ndi.video/tools/)

*free software, NDI, signal testing* <br>

Tools for NDI video sources. Can be used to test which NDI signals are present on the network or to share your computer or webcam through NDI.

---

## VLC

The gold standard in simple video playback. Plays almost every type of video.

## [Eagle Animation](https://brickfilms.com/eagle-animation) *by BrickFilms*

*open source software, stop motion animation, capture*

Simple and easy cross-platform software for capturing a webcam feed into a frame-by-frame animation. Has features like onion skinning and simple controls for exposure and cropping.

---

## BOATS Animator

*open source software, stop motion animation, video capture*

Simple and easy cross-platform software for capturing a webcam feed into a frame-by-frame animation. A bit simpler than Eagle Animation, but still capable.

---

## Wire *by Resolume*
Wire is the engine behind Arena/Avenues effects and generative sources. It is a full node-based programming environment that can run stand-alone or make and edit effects and generators for use in Arena/Avenue.
